Sun finder is a simple dual axis solar tracking system. Sun finder has two forms of tracking: full sweep and adjustment. Full sweep will set it to a default tilt angle, then perform a full 360 pan, checking at every interval the power output and recording that with the position. After completing a full swivel, it turns to the position of the greatest output. From there, it begins adjustment. Adjustment will compare two panels and turn towards the side with the greater output until the difference is within a threshold. It then continues, counting each step, until it cross the threshold from the other side, then returning to the middle of those two threshold points. The system chooses whether to adjust the pan or tilt first based on which direction has the greater output initially.
